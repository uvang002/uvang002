# Hi, I'm Union

Electrical Engineering student at Butte College and a Respiratory Therapist with **9+ years of experience** in patient care, ranging from patient assessment, treatment, and emergency response to managing mechanical ventilators. I am transitioning the critical thinking, time management, and closed-loop communication skills I developed in healthcare into **FPGA and Digital Hardware Design**.

My experience in acute care shares many parallels with engineering. It taught me how to think systematically, troubleshoot complex problems, and remain calm under pressure while maintaining professionalism. The tools and applications may be different, but the commitment to understanding complex systems and solving problems remains unchanged. Today, I apply that same rigor to digital logic design, developing self-checking testbenches and verifying hardware behavior through simulation and analysis.

Rather than relying solely on vendor GUI tools, I build and verify HDL designs from the ground up using **OSS CAD Suite** and other open-source development tools. This hands-on approach has given me a deeper understanding of digital design workflows, from Verilog HDL development and verification to synthesis. Just as understanding the respiratory system is essential to being an effective Respiratory Therapist, understanding the underlying tools and workflows is essential to becoming a competent hardware engineer.

My goal is not only to learn FPGA development but also to document the journey in a way that helps other students and career changers navigate the same path with greater confidence and fewer barriers.
